# Quant Research

Self-contained quantitative finance projects, each with its own code, tests, results and write-up.

| # | Project | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 01 | [Fat-Tail Risk & the Hill Estimator](01-tail-risk/) | How fat are the tails of real market returns, and how badly does a Gaussian VaR miss them? |
| 02 | [Option Greeks with Monte Carlo & JAX](02-greeks-autodiff/) | How do Monte Carlo bump-and-revalue and autodiff estimate option Greeks, and why does pathwise gamma fail? |

Each project folder has a `README.md` with the method and results, and a `requirements.txt`.
